# The Copper Ladle — a hectic kitchen rush

A fast-paced cooking game: you play a chef running a small kitchen, grabbing ingredients, chopping, grilling, and assembling dishes to match customer orders before their tickets expire.

## How to play

Move with WASD or the arrow keys (or click a station to walk there) and press E to use a station — pick up, chop, grill, plate, and serve.

## Recipes and scoring

Four recipes to master: Garden Salad, Melty Toast, Copper Burger, and the Onion Stack (unlocked once the shift gets rolling). Chain correct serves to build a combo for bonus tips, and survive the lunch rush as orders arrive faster with less patience.
